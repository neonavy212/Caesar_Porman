Business Thinking, Personal Growth

About Me

I teach International Baccalaureate students across two complementary areas: Business Management, where students analyze organizations and make evidence-based decisions, and Personal and Professional Skills, where students develop the reflective, interpersonal, career-readiness, and self-management skills needed beyond school.

My classroom practice combines real-world business cases, explicit assessment literacy, inquiry, structured feedback, digital portfolios, and educational technology. I make expectations visible, break complex tasks into manageable steps, and help students produce evidence of both academic understanding and personal growth.

Strong learning is not only about the final answer — it is also about the thinking, reflection, evidence, and decisions that lead there.
